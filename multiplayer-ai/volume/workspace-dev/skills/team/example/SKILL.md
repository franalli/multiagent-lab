# Team skill (example team)

Per-team knowledge that belongs to a specific team but not the whole company.

## Rituals
- Daily standup Monday/Wednesday/Friday at 09:30 CET (15 min, async OK).
- Demo-friday rotation -- one engineer presents what shipped that week.

## Tooling
- This team uses Linear for tickets, not Jira.
- Code reviews require two approvals for merges to `main`.
